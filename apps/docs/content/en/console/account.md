Your account is under **Settings** › **Account**: choose **Account** in the menu at the foot of the sidebar.

| Page | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Profile | Picture, name, email address, account id, and deleting the account |
| Security | Password, two-factor authentication and recovery codes |
| Sessions | Where you are signed in |
| API keys | Your keys across every project |
| Memberships | Your organizations and projects, with your roles; leaving an organization |
| Notifications | Which emails you receive |
| Appearance | Theme and language |

## Email address

**Change email** sends a confirmation link to the new address and tells the old one. The change takes effect when you open the link. It asks for a recent sign-in.

## Password and two-factor authentication

**Change password** signs out your other sessions. If you have no password because you sign in with a link or single sign-on, **Set password** adds one.

Two-factor authentication uses an authenticator app and applies to password sign-in; sign-in links need no code. **Set up** shows a QR code and a setup key; enter the six-digit code from the app and choose **Turn on**. You get 10 recovery codes, each usable once instead of a code, shown only then; copy or download them and store them somewhere safe. **Regenerate** replaces them, and **Turn off** removes two-factor authentication.

If you lose both your authenticator and your recovery codes, an instance admin can reset your two-factor authentication.

## Sessions

**Sessions** lists every browser where you are signed in, with how you signed in. **End session** signs one out; **Sign out everywhere** signs out all but this one. Sessions end on their own after 7 days without use, and after 30 days at most.

## Leaving and deleting

**Leave organization**, under **Memberships**, removes you from the organization and its projects and archives your keys there. **Delete account**, under **Profile**, removes you from every organization and archives all your keys; you confirm by typing your email address, and it asks for a recent sign-in. Neither works while you are an organization's last owner: make someone else an owner first.

## Notifications

**Budget alerts** and **Expiring keys** turn those emails on or off. Security emails, such as a changed password, are always sent.

## Theme and language

Under **Appearance**:

- **Theme**: **System**, **Light** or **Dark**. It is kept in this browser.
- **Language**: **Automatic**, English, Deutsch or 简体中文. Automatic follows your browser's language and falls back to English. Your choice is saved to your account, so it follows you to other browsers, and emails from Kaeso use it too.

Product names and model ids stay as they are in every language.
