The console runs at your install's domain, such as `https://kaeso.example.com`. Sign in with your email address; [sign-in and single sign-on](../self-hosting/sign-in.md) explains what the sign-in page offers.

- [Organizations and projects](organizations-and-projects.md): Create organizations and projects, connect single sign-on, and manage their settings.
- [Members and roles](members-and-roles.md): Invite people, give them roles and rights, and create service accounts.
- [Provider keys](provider-keys.md): Connect your keys for model providers, and choose which projects use each one.
- [Custom providers](custom-providers.md): Add a server of your own, such as a self-hosted vLLM or an internal proxy, as a provider.
- [Models and aliases](models-and-aliases.md): Choose the models a project may call, and give them names of your own.
- [API keys](api-keys.md): Create keys for apps and scripts, limit them, and turn them off.
- [Limits and budgets](limits-and-budgets.md): Cap the requests, tokens and spending of a project, a member or a key.
- [Request logs](request-logs.md): Find any request, export logs, and choose what is kept.
- [Playground](playground.md): Compare up to four models side by side.
- [Account and language](account.md): Secure your account, and choose your theme and language.
- [Instance administration](instance-administration.md): Manage the users, organizations, settings and updates of the install.

## Find your way around

The sidebar shows the organization's pages, or a project's when you work in one. The menu at its foot switches organization and opens **Organization settings**, your **Account** and, for instance admins, **Instance**. What the sidebar lists depends on your [rights](members-and-roles.md).

Press ⌘K, or Ctrl+K, to search pages, keys, models and members, to create things, or to switch organization or project. When no field has focus, single keys work too, such as `c` to create on the current page or `g` `k` to go to API keys. Press `?` to list every shortcut.

A list, such as API keys or members, is a single Tab stop. Move between its rows with the arrow keys or `j` and `k`, and open one with Enter.
